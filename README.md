# Assignment Guide

*Understand your assignment. Develop your own solution.*

**Course access required:** Assignment Guide is for students on participating HKU courses. You’ll need a personal course key supplied by your teaching team.

Get step-by-step guidance on your programming assignments in VS Code. Ask questions
about your code, work through problems with hints, and review suggested changes
before applying them.

## Get started

1. Install **Assignment Guide** (`alandougherty.assignment-guide`) supplied by your teacher.
2. Open your assignment folder in VS Code.
3. Open the Command Palette and run **Assignment Guide: Connect to Your Course**.
   Enter your personal course key supplied by your teacher.
4. Follow the prompts to review the recording notice and confirm your identity
   and assignment.

The tutor opens beside your editor. Use **Assignment Guide: Open** to return to it.
If you need a course key, ask your teacher.

## Ask for help

Type a question and press **Enter** to send. Use **Shift+Enter** for a new line.
The tutor can use eligible files in your assignment folder, including unsaved
editor changes, to understand your question. Try asking for a hint, an explanation
or feedback on your approach.

When your course provides a token allowance, the remaining balance appears beside
the request buttons. Hover over it for details. A last-known balance may be out of
date; an unavailable balance means the service cannot give an exact count.

## Review suggested changes

Ask for an edit in your question. If the tutor proposes a change, choose
**Review change** to inspect it, then **Accept change** or **Reject change**.
Accepted changes update and save your file. You remain
responsible for checking your work. AI guidance can be wrong.

## Assignment 2

Version 0.19.0 adds the Assignment 2 tutor. Open the assignment folder supplied
by your teacher and use your course key. Your conversation returns when you
reconnect. Questions saved while offline are sent when the connection returns.
Use **Start over** for a new conversation; earlier conversations stay available
to read and export. Assignment 1 history remains available too.

## Your questions and work

Submitted questions, captured assignment files, tutor replies and edit decisions
are recorded locally and by your course service. Read your course's recording
notice before you begin. Do not include passwords or personal information in
assignment files. Follow your course's guidance on what you may submit.

Use **Export chat** to save a copy of your conversation for your records. Follow
your teacher's instructions about submissions.

## Need help?

If the tutor asks you to reconnect, use **Reconnect to course** and check your
identity. For additional troubleshooting controls, run **Assignment Guide:
Toggle diagnostics** from the Command Palette. Run it again to hide them.

See the [student guide](https://github.com/Alandougherty/assignment-guide/blob/main/docs/student-guide.md) for detailed instructions.
